## Executive Summary

The client website **Roofing Specialist** (`roofingspecialist.ie`) is an active WordPress-based Irish roofing contractor website serving **Dublin, Kildare, Wicklow, Meath, and Wexford**. 

The site is built using the **Breakdance Builder** on WordPress with an Nginx web server. The business represents master roofers with over 40 years of combined experience offering 24/7 emergency roof repairs, new roof installations, flat roofing (torch-on felt, fibreglass GRP, EPDM), chimney restoration, and uPVC guttering.

Key legacy branding signals reveal that the company previously operated or traded under the name **"Roof Guard"** (`roofguard.ie`), as evidenced by asset filenames, URL slugs (e.g. `/expert-roofers-dublin-kildare-wexford-roof-guard/`), image alt attributes, and schema organization logo files.

## 5. Roofing Services Catalog & Descriptions

- **Page Endpoint:** `https://roofingspecialist.ie/roof-repairs-dublin/`
- **Key Offerings:**
  - 24/7 fast emergency callouts across Dublin and Leinster.
  - Active roof leak diagnosis and immediate water stoppage.
  - Storm damage repair, temporary weather-tight tarpaulin installation.
  - Replacement of slipped, cracked, or missing natural slates and concrete tiles.
  - Valley gutter repairs and ridge tile re-bedding and repointing.
  - Internal ceiling leak investigation and rafter inspection.

### 5.2 New Roof Installation & Replacement
- **Page Endpoint:** `https://roofingspecialist.ie/roof-installation-dublin/` (also `/new-roof-installation/`)
- **Key Offerings:**
  - Full structural re-roofing for older, failing roofs.
  - New builds and domestic extension roofing.
  - Timber roof framing, truss replacement, and rotten timber repairs.
  - High-performance breathable underlay membranes and treated battens.
  - Natural slate roofs (Tegral, Welsh slate, Spanish slate) and interlocking concrete/clay tiles.
  - Lead valley gutters and chimney lead aprons.
  - Comprehensive multi-year written guarantees on new roof structures.

### 5.3 Flat Roof Specialists (Installation & Repair)
- **Page Endpoint:** `https://roofingspecialist.ie/flat-roof-install-repair/`
- **Key Offerings:**
  - Over 40 years of dedicated flat roof expertise.
  - **Torch-On Felt Roofing:** 3-layer elastomeric bitumen felt systems with mineral finish.
  - **Fibreglass GRP Flat Roofing:** Seamless, impact-resistant liquid fibreglass roofing with zero joints.
  - **EPDM Rubber Roofing:** Durable single-ply rubber membrane installations.
  - Applications: Kitchen extensions, dormer roofs, residential garages, porch canopies, commercial flat roofs.
  - Integrated insulation upgrades (warm roof construction) and skylight / roof lantern curb waterproofing.

### 5.4 Expert Chimney Repairs
- **Page Endpoint:** `https://roofingspecialist.ie/chimney-repairs-dublin/`
- **Key Offerings:**
  - Chimney stack repointing and mortar restoration.
  - Complete chimney stack structural rebuilds and take-downs.
  - Chimney pot replacement, re-bedding, and bird guard / cowl installation.
  - Concrete chimney crown (flaunching) repair and waterproofing.
  - Step, back-gutter, and apron lead flashing replacement to stop chimney-line leaks.

### 5.5 Guttering, Fascias & Soffits
- **Page Endpoint:** `https://roofingspecialist.ie/guttering-dublin/`
- **Key Offerings:**
  - Installation and repair of uPVC and seamless aluminium gutters.
  - Gutter leak sealing, bracket realignment, and pitch correction for optimal drainage.
  - High-pressure gutter cleaning, leaf debris clearing, and downpipe unblocking.
  - Replacement of rotted timber fascias and soffits with durable uPVC capping boards.
  - Dry verge cap installations on roof gable edges (eliminating mortar maintenance).

### 5.6 Commercial Roofing
- **Page Endpoint:** Detailed in guides (`/commercial-roofing-dublin/`)

  - Commercial flat roofing systems and preventative waterproofing.
