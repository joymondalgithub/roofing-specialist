About us

Roofing Specialist

Roofing Specialist brings together master roofers with over 40 years of combined experience in the Irish roofing trade. Based in Dublin 8, we provide durable, high-standard roofing solutions across Dublin, Kildare, Wicklow, Meath, and Wexford.

We work with homeowners, property managers, and commercial clients on everything from minor leak repairs and slipped slates to complete re-roofing, torch-on flat roofing, and chimney rebuilds. Every roofer on our team is Safe Pass certified, and all work is fully insured and guaranteed.
